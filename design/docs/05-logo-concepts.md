# Ariamena — Logo concepts

## Direction 1 — Stratified A (selected)
An "A" built from three stacked strata: an apex, a crossbar band, and two feet, separated by two thin gaps.
- Read top-down it is one letter. Read bottom-up it is the story: human experience, structure, intelligence.
- Construction: 100-unit grid. Outer slope from (50,8) to (10,90). Gaps of 6 units. Feet notched to form the counter.
- Holds at 16 px. Single color. No gradient, no glow. Favicon-safe.

## Direction 2 — AM monogram
Aria and Mena as one continuous stroke. The A's right leg lands where the M's first stem rises, so the two words share a stroke.
- Reads as a signal trace at small sizes and a monogram at large ones.
- Wide format; needs 24 px minimum height. Best as a header lockup on light editorial layouts.

## Direction 3 — Wordmark with a data point
Lowercase "ariamena" in Instrument Sans 600, −4.5% tracking, closed by a small square: the data point at the end of a human word.
- The square takes the section's accent (cobalt on light, cyan on dark) or the text color in one-color use.
- Optional below 14 px.

## Final logo system
- **Mark:** Stratified A.
- **Wordmark:** lowercase "ariamena" with the square.
- **Lockup:** mark + wordmark, gap of half the cap height, mark height equal to the x-height plus ascender.
- **Color:** ink (#1C1A17) on light, off-white (#F4EFE7) on dark. One color only. Never on gradients that reduce contrast.
- **Clear space:** the height of the apex triangle on all sides.
- **Don'ts:** no outline version, no gradient fill, no rotation, no separation of the strata into different colors.
