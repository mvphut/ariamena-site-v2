### 01 — Human

H2: Every intelligent system begins with people.

The nuance of real work lives in hands, timing, judgment, and space. A technician hears a machine change pitch. A teacher reads a room. A nurse adjusts before the chart says to. None of that exists in a spreadsheet. Ariamena works with people in the places where this knowledge is used, so it can be captured with its context intact.

### 02 — Data

H2: Reality becomes a usable signal.

Observation is not yet data. Between the two sits careful work: deciding what to capture, how to describe it, and how to know it is right. Ariamena runs that work as one accountable process.

States: Raw scene — What a person sees / Data layer — What is observed / Structured — What a model learns from

- **Capture.** Record activity in the environment where it happens, with the consent and coverage the model needs.

- **Organize.** Sort raw material into sessions, scenes, and sequences with clean metadata.

- **Label.** Add the structure a model can learn from: boundaries, keypoints, transcripts, intent, outcome.

- **Validate.** Check quality, consistency, and relevance against agreed acceptance criteria.

- **Deliver.** Hand over documented, versioned, model-ready data with clear provenance.

### 04 — Method

H2: From the field to the model.

Five stages. Each one is a layer of the same system, and each locks into the one before it.

- **01 Design the data program** (Human experience). We start with what the model must understand, then define the environments, activities, coverage, and acceptance criteria that will get it there.

- **02 Capture real-world signals** (Observation). Trained contributors record activity in real settings, using the modalities the task calls for: video, audio, sensor, spatial, and written context.

- **03 Structure and annotate** (Context). Raw material becomes organized, labeled data: boundaries, keypoints, sequences, transcripts, and the metadata that keeps it meaningful.

- **04 Validate for quality and relevance** (Structure). Multi-stage review checks accuracy and consistency, and confirms the data still answers the original question.

- **05 Deliver data ready for AI development** (Intelligence). Documented, versioned, and traceable datasets, delivered in the format your team builds with.

Layers: Human experience → Observation → Context → Structure → Intelligence

### 05 — Responsibility

H2: Human data requires human responsibility.

AI should not lose the people behind the data. Ariamena designs responsible data programs around context, care, quality, and clear operational standards.

- **Privacy-aware by design.** Programs are scoped to what the model needs and no more. Sensitive material is minimized, protected, and handled according to agreed rules.

- **Consent-conscious workflows.** Contributors know what is being captured, why, and how it will be used, before capture begins.

- **Clear governance.** Every program has defined owners, permitted uses, and handling standards, documented from the start.

- **Quality and traceability.** Each data point can be traced to its origin, its review history, and its acceptance status.

- **Context before scale.** A smaller dataset that represents reality is worth more than a larger one that flattens it.

- **Respect for people and places.** The people and environments behind the data are partners in the work, not raw material.

### 06 — Outcome

H2: AI that understands more. Because it starts with more.

When AI learns from genuine context, it can make better sense of the work, spaces, decisions, and people it is designed to support.

- **Generalizes to the real setting.** Systems built on data from the environments they will work in behave more predictably there.

- **Fewer surprises after the lab.** The gap between a benchmark and a shift on the floor narrows when the floor was in the data.

- **Respects the people it learned from.** Provenance, consent, and context travel with the data, so the model's origins stay accountable.

## Responsible Data

H1: Human data requires human responsibility.

Ariamena describes its practices plainly. We do not claim certifications we have not earned or make promises the work cannot keep. What follows is how programs are designed and run.

**Privacy-aware by design.** Programs are scoped to what the model needs and no more. Sensitive material is minimized, protected, and handled according to agreed rules.

**Consent-conscious workflows.** Contributors know what is being captured, why, and how it will be used, before capture begins.

**Clear governance.** Every program has defined owners, permitted uses, and handling standards, documented from the start.

**Quality and traceability.** Each data point can be traced to its origin, its review history, and its acceptance status.

**Context before scale.** A smaller dataset that represents reality is worth more than a larger one that flattens it.

**Respect for people and places.** The people and environments behind the data are partners in the work, not raw material.

**In practice**

- **Before capture.** Scope is agreed and written down: what is captured, where, from whom, for what permitted use. Contributors are briefed and consent is recorded before any recording begins.

- **During capture.** Sensitive material is minimized at the source. Contributors can pause or withdraw. Program owners are reachable throughout.

- **During structuring and review.** Access is limited to the people doing the work. Every data point carries its origin and review history.

- **At delivery and after.** Permitted uses travel with the data. Retention and deletion follow the agreed terms. Documentation is delivered with every version.

## About

H1: A company built on the idea that AI should understand the world it works in.

Ariamena exists because useful AI cannot be trained on abstraction alone. It needs real human context, real environments, responsible collection, and rigorous structure.

**The name.** Ariamena joins two ideas. Aria: a single human voice, given form and heard clearly. Mena: the people and places the company comes from. Together they describe the work: human knowledge, made legible for intelligent systems, without losing the people behind it.

- **Context is the product.** A recording without its setting, sequence, and intent is not yet useful. We capture the context with the signal.

- **People are partners.** Contributors are briefed, consented, trained, and credited in the program's records. They are not a crowd.

- **Rigor is a form of respect.** Acceptance criteria, review, and traceability protect the people behind the data as much as the model built from it.

- **Quiet claims, clear evidence.** We would rather show a method than announce a number.

**Work with us.** Ariamena is early-stage and growing. We are interested in hearing from people who know real environments from the inside, from operations leaders who want AI that understands their work, and from research and engineering partners who want a data partner rather than a vendor.
