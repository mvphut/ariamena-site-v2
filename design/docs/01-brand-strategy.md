# Ariamena — Brand strategy and art direction

## Positioning

**Ariamena builds the human data layer that teaches AI how the real world works.**

Category: human data infrastructure for real-world AI.
Not a crowd marketplace. Not a labeling shop. Not a consultancy. Ariamena designs and runs data programs inside real environments, with the people who work in them, and delivers structured, validated, model-ready data with clear provenance.

Supporting lines (used sparingly across the site):
- From real life to reliable intelligence.
- Teaching AI the nuance of the real world.
- Human reality, structured for intelligent systems.

The one-sentence promise a visitor should leave with:
"Useful AI cannot be trained on abstraction alone. Ariamena is the partner that brings real human context, real environments, responsible collection, and rigorous structure."

## Brand personality

The calm expert in the room. A field researcher crossed with a systems engineer.

| Trait | What it looks like | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| Observant | Notices the detail that matters: the hand, the pause, the handover | Voyeuristic, surveillance-toned |
| Rigorous | Defines acceptance criteria before capture starts | Bureaucratic, jargon-heavy |
| Humane | Treats contributors as partners, not raw material | Soft, lifestyle, sentimental |
| Quietly confident | Lets clarity do the persuading | Loud, hype-driven, metric-flexing |
| Grounded | Speaks in concrete nouns: floors, shifts, classrooms, tools | Abstract "AI future" language |

## Voice and tone

- Calm, precise, plain-spoken. Short headlines. One clarifying sentence. Stop.
- Concrete over abstract. "A technician hears a machine change pitch" beats "domain expertise".
- People, not users or crowd. Programs, not projects. Contributors, not workers.
- Honest scope. We describe practices, never certifications, numbers, or clients we cannot back.
- Editorial rhythm: eyebrow, headline, one paragraph, evidence.
- Banned words: revolutionary, game-changing, unlock, seamlessly, cutting-edge, leverage, supercharge, "the future is here".

## Visual principles

1. **Evidence, not illustration.** Visuals show the act of observation: frames, keypoints, boundaries, sequences. Nothing decorative.
2. **One axis carries the story.** Warm human light on one end, cool technical depth on the other. Every page sits somewhere on that axis.
3. **Restraint at scale.** Very large type, very small details, a great deal of air between them.
4. **Lines over blobs.** Hairlines, grids, and strokes build structure. Glow is never structure.
5. **One accent at a time.** Cobalt does the work. Cyan and ultraviolet appear only at the "intelligence" end of the story. One warm accent stays with the human.

## Motion principles

1. **Motion explains transformation.** Every animation moves something from human to data, or from data back to human.
2. **Scroll is the timeline.** Scroll position drives narrative scenes. Scrolling is never hijacked or slowed.
3. **Camera, not bounce.** Eases are long and asymmetric (fast-out, slow-in). No overshoot, no spring.
4. **UI under 600 ms.** Reveals and hovers are quick. Only narrative scenes are scroll-linked.
5. **Reduced motion is a first-class state.** Every scene has a complete static final frame. Content never depends on animation.

## Typography recommendation

| Role | Family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Display and body | Instrument Sans | Contemporary grotesk with a slight warmth; tight display setting; four weights |
| Human accent | Instrument Serif Italic | Same superfamily; a single italic serif word inside a headline marks the human moment |
| Data labels | JetBrains Mono | Narrow, legible at 11–12 px, reads as instrumentation, not "code" |

Scale: fluid, clamp-based. Display 56–120 px, H2 40–72 px, body 17–19 px, labels 11–12 px uppercase with 0.08 em tracking.

## Accessibility principles

- Body text at 4.5:1 minimum, large text at 3:1, including text over gradients and dark scenes.
- Visible focus rings on every interactive element, in the accent color, never removed.
- Full keyboard navigation, including the mobile menu and the industry selector.
- Semantic landmarks, one H1 per page, ordered headings, labeled controls.
- 44 px minimum touch targets. Skip link on every page.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables parallax, pinned scenes, and continuous animation; final states remain.
- Meaning is never carried by color alone. Every data overlay has a text label.
