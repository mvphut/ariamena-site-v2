Why this fits Ariamena

Strength. Immediately reads as AI infrastructure to labs and robotics teams. Data overlays are naturally at home on dark ground, so the "observation" visual language is strongest here.

Risk. Dark-first sites in this category are common, and the human half of the story is harder to feel. Warmth must come from typography and copy alone.

Best for. A brand that wants to be seen primarily as a technical layer, with the human story told in words rather than in light.
