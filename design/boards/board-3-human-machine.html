The whole page is one crossing. The background moves along the warm-to-dark axis with scroll. The figure is drawn once and re-rendered: warm silhouette, keypoints, structured frame, then silhouette again inside the model's field. Layers lock in a pinned stack in the Method section. Ease: cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1).

parallax 3 depths · pinned scene only in Method · reduced motion: each section shows its final frame

Why this fits Ariamena

Strength. It is the company's sentence made visible: human → data → AI → better outcomes for people. No other direction can tell that story in the background color alone. It carries both credibility signals at once: warmth for the people and rigor for the labs.

Risk. The transition must be engineered carefully so mid-axis sections keep contrast. Every component needs a light and a dark state, which raises build discipline.

Best for. Ariamena. The story is the differentiator; this system makes the story structural rather than decorative.

contrast · body #4A453E on #F4EFE7 = 8.1:1 ✓ · #A5ACBA on #0B0C0F = 8.6:1 ✓ · sienna on off-white = 5.0:1 ✓
