Elements draft in like figures in a paper: a hairline draws, a label types, a keypoint settles. No parallax; instead, staggered reveals with 60 ms offsets. Diagrams animate on intersection once, then hold. Ease: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1).

no continuous motion · reveals ≤ 500 ms · reduced motion: instant

Why this fits Ariamena

Strength. The most trustworthy of the three. Research organizations and enterprise buyers read light editorial systems as credible and calm. The human story is easy to feel in warm neutrals.

Risk. Without the technical depth of a dark end, the "structured intelligence" half can feel understated. The site relies on diagram craft to prove technical seriousness.

Best for. A brand that wants to be seen as the thoughtful, rigorous partner first, and as infrastructure second.
